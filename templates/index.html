Medical Imaging AI Research Demo

AI-Powered Chest X-Ray Screening

MedScan Assist evaluates deep convolutional transfer learning architectures (EfficientNet) for detecting radiographic signs of pneumonia with interpretable Grad-CAM saliency heatmaps and image quality assessment.

Start Screening Demo Methodology & Limitations
Transfer Learning Analysis

Evaluates frontal chest radiographs using an EfficientNet backbone fine-tuned to distinguish normal parenchyma from pulmonary opacities.

Grad-CAM Interpretability

Computes Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping to visually highlight pulmonary regions influencing the neural network's decision.

Pre-Analysis Quality Checks

Inspects image resolution, dynamic range, and blur metrics to flag degraded inputs before inferencing, mitigating misleading artifacts.

Specifications & Input Data

Supported Imaging Modality

To ensure valid model feature extraction, input studies must conform to the standard frontal chest projection specifications outlined below:

Projection

Frontal Chest Radiographs: Anteroposterior (AP) or Posteroanterior (PA) projections only. Lateral views are not supported.

File Formats

Standard digital image formats: JPEG (.jpg, .jpeg) and PNG (.png). File sizes up to 10 MB.

Patient Demographics Notice

Underlying training data was derived from pediatric cohort chest radiographs (1 to 5 years of age). Generalization across adult cohorts may exhibit performance variance.
